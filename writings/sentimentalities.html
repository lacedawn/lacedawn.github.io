sentimentalities

october 24, 2026

you could talk about sentimentalities and “leaving a place in the world” all you want while spreading all the useless, irrelevant and unnecessary info into an already oversaturated system where there’s hardly anything of value to engage with, in order to discover, to link and connect and create more ideas, attempting as hard as possible, as efficiently as possible, to squeeze something you could wave around proudly, happily. “I made this!! It was me!! My name is to be cemented!!”, but overlooking the temporary nature of everything and the accelerating pattern of sweeping out all humanisms, sentimentalities, nostalgia, in favor of a far more advanced system of efficiency and speed, it’s just futile. anthropocentrism is a disease that has plagued all stages of society, if the human is, really, this advanced, last-step-of-intelligence, king of all beings, then the objective of man is to overcome himself. if, since the dawn of time, human effort has always been directed into improving the efficiency of all things around him, why not look into the mirror for a second, to realize that what has always been overlooked, what holds the potential for an ever more advanced system of efficiency, is right at hand. it’s the egoism of human consciousness that stands in the way of admitting that the biological reality of humans with all its related limits has always been the main obstacle to be overcome. religion is deeply anthropocentric, holding that existence was set up in order for man to step in and make use of all its properties, affirming that all animals other than man are there purely for human satisfaction and enjoyment. that the natural elements of beauty, chaos, elegance, sophistication of existence are purely for man to come and observe them. religion is the most sophisticated tool of human pamperment. intelligence, too, which is the governor of all advancements, improvement and evolution, has been a victim of anthropocentrism: dumbed down to mere numbers which reflect the exclusively human ability to recognize patterns, to solve puzzles. this is perhaps the vilest exploitation caused by this disease, to reduce the governor of all existence to IQ. it is like a one-sided argument in which man excitedly announces his own victory, that is the ownership of intelligence. though intelligence is utterly oblivious of this, it doesn’t care about such arguments, it’s far too mechanical to pay them any attention, to let a thing like this stagger its efficiency. culture is an aspect of anthropocentrism in which man assigns arbitrary value to the old while overlooking the fact that the preservation of culture itself necessitates the constant replacement of the old. preservation proceeds by restoration: rebuilt walls, recopied manuscripts, each pass swapping the original for a representation of it, until what remains of the culture is an image of the item, a symbol. it’s a confusion man’s biology walks him into again and again without ever learning from his supposed pattern recognition abilities. religion, culture, IQ all belong to one mechanism which anthropocentrism runs, and they are not to be mistaken for separate aspects of human life, each with its own properties and functions. Biology is the ruler of man, not intelligence.

dissolution is the beauty of all beauties, the aesthetic of breakdown, the elegance of replacement, or dissolution for the sake of dissolution, the urgent need to abandon outdated systems in favor of an open horizon of possibilities and connections where vague transcendent concepts of knowledge, existence and law itself are rendered obsolete, and gradually, unthinkable. the dissolution in which old habits and instincts are intelligence in an underdeveloped form. the abolition of all that requires occasional contemplation of the past.
